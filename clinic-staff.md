# For clinic and front-desk staff

For front-desk staff, practice managers, and anyone who runs check-in at a clinic.

## Background

Today, patients often fill out the same forms at every visit. With SMART Health Check-in, the clinic's check-in page, portal, or front-desk kiosk asks the patient's own health app for what it needs: demographics, insurance, allergies, medications, or a short form. The patient sees exactly what's being asked, chooses what to share, and the answers arrive at the clinic ready to review.

At this connectathon, clinics' software and patients' health apps are trying this together for the first time. You know what a real front desk needs, so your view of whether this would fit is some of the most useful feedback we can get.

## Get started

All the demos run in a web browser. The patients, records, and clinics are made up.

1. **Check a patient in at a kiosk.** Open the [kiosk demo](https://smart-health-checkin.org/client/demo/kiosk.html) on a computer, as the front-desk screen. Scan its QR code with a phone, or use the link under the code if you have no second device. On the phone, choose **Demo wallet** and share. The kiosk then shows the check-in as done, with what arrived.
2. **See what the patient sees.** Try the [clinic check-in demo](https://smart-health-checkin.org/client/demo/#wallet=demo) on a phone or computer: press **Check in with Demo wallet**, choose what to share, and press **Share selected**.
3. **See a form filled in from a health app.** The [allergy form demo](https://smart-health-checkin.org/client/demo/autofill.html#wallet=demo) fills in a form from what the patient shares.
4. **Think about your front desk.** What would you trust as it arrives, what would you still check, and what would get in the way on a busy morning?

The [kiosk flow explainer](https://smart-health-checkin.org/spec/kiosk-flow-explainer.html) describes the hand-off from kiosk to phone in more detail.
